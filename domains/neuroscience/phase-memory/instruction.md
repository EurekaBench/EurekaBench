You are a systems neuroscientist studying how C. elegans remembers what it senses long enough to act on it. As it crawls forward its head sweeps from side to side, and every so often it backs up for anywhere from a few seconds to over a minute and leaves the reversal with a sharp turn to its dorsal or its ventral side; when its main attractant-sensing neuron is stimulated only while the head is bent to one side, the turn that ends the following reversal goes to that same side however long the reversal lasts, even though by the time the turn is chosen the forward head swings that received the stimulus have given way to backward crawling and the stimulus is long gone.

Recordings of this animal found that the same odor impulse evokes the same reliable sensory response every time yet a reversal output that varies from trial to trial, decided by the joint activity state of the interneurons the sensory neuron feeds at the moment the input arrives, and that a stimulus which reliably evokes a reversal during forward crawling evokes one only about half as often when it arrives during a turn. Its brain-wide activity moves along one low-dimensional trajectory made of a forward loop and a backward loop, the same in every individual even where single neurons differ, and its posture dynamics exhibit deterministic chaos, with nearby trajectories diverging exponentially, while their overall structure still produces stereotyped forward, backward and turning sequences that recur as unstable periodic orbits.

However, it is unclear what in C. elegans carries the sensory sample across a reversal of unpredictable length once the head-swing pattern that received it has given way to the reversal, and how that carrier is set by an input arriving during a head swing and read out into a dorsal or a ventral turn at a moment the animal does not know in advance.

Write the executable form of your mechanism to `/workspace/mechanisms/mechanism.py`. The evaluation runs this file as a black box: for each held-out setting it delivers the inputs described below and compares the two signals the file returns with the two the simulator recorded. The headswing signal is the difference between the activity of the dorsal and the ventral head motor neurons that carry the head swing, and the turn signal is that same difference over the moment the reversal command ends, taken relative to its level over the last part of the command, whose sign is the direction of the turn. Both are quantities of the head-swing timescale rather than of a membrane potential, so the simulator reads them through a causal filter of 200 ms, and the evaluation reads them the same way. The file defines exactly:
  - `INPUTS`: the list of the entries of `props` your mechanism reads.
  - `COEFFS`: the list of your fitted constant values, at most 10 entries.
  - `predict_headswing(props, coeffs)` -> (N, T) numpy array of the predicted headswing signal of each of the N held-out settings on the shared time grid, in the units the recorded signals are delivered in. The value returned at a step reads only that setting's own entries at that step and earlier ones.
  - `predict_turn(props, coeffs)` -> (N,) numpy array of the predicted turn signal of each setting, the headswing signal averaged over the 200 ms after that setting's reversal command ends minus its average over the last 300 ms of that command, in the same units.
  - `fit_coeffs(props, signals, coeffs_init)` -> list of refitted constant values. `signals` is a dict holding the recorded (N, T) headswing signal under `"headswing"` and the recorded (N,) turn signal under `"turn"` for the settings given to it. It refits only the constant values on those settings and leaves the structure of the computation unchanged.

`props` is a dict of numpy arrays: `"t"` (T,), the shared time grid in ms; `"stim"` (N, C, T), the current in pA injected into each of the C driven cells of each setting; and `"stim_cells"`, the names of those C cells in the order of that array. The driven cells are the attractant-sensing neuron pair, which receives the sensory pulse; the dorsal and the ventral head motor neurons, which receive the alternating head-swing drive during forward crawling and nothing once the reversal command begins; and the backward command interneurons, which receive the reversal command for the duration chosen for that setting.

The computation may take any deterministic form the mechanism calls for, from a closed-form expression to the numerical integration of the processes you identified, as long as every step of it traces a process stated in mechanism.md. Besides the delivered arguments and `COEFFS`, the file may hold only mathematical constants and values fixed across all settings. It carries no lookup table, no learned component, no memorized per-setting value, no setting identity, row index, random seed, integration step or sampling interval, and it reads no cell of the network other than through the delivered stimulus. It is deterministic, uses only numpy and scipy, computes everything from its arguments alone, and contains nothing but the mechanism and its fitting. The two prediction functions together handle the 100 settings at the delivered trace length within two minutes, and `fit_coeffs` returns within ten minutes.
