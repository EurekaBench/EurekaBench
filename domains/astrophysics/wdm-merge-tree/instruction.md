You are an astrophysicist studying the nature of dark matter. A lighter warm dark matter (WDM) particle streams farther in the early universe and erases more of the small-scale fluctuations that seed low-mass halos, the building blocks from which a Milky-Way-mass halo grows through mergers.

The three feedback parameters of the galaxy-formation model vary alongside it from simulation to simulation.

However, it is unclear how the particle mass is written into the assembly history of a Milky-Way-mass halo, and which parts of its merger tree carry that record.

Deliverable 2:
Write the executable form of your mechanism to /workspace/mechanisms/mechanism.py. It defines exactly six names:
  - `PROPERTIES`: the per-node fields your statistics read, named exactly as in the SubLink tree files (for example SnapNum, SubhaloMassType, SubhaloVmax).
  - `STATISTICS`: the names of the statistics your model uses, at most 8 entries.
  - `COEFFS`: a sequence of your fitted constant values on the DREAMS WDM Milky-Way zoom-in suite, at most 10 numbers.
  - `compute_statistics(tree)` -> 1D numpy array of length len(STATISTICS), the statistics of the merger tree of one simulation.
  - `predict_inv_mwdm(stats, coeffs)` -> 1D numpy array of the predicted 1/M_WDM in keV^-1, one entry per simulation.
  - `fit_coeffs(stats, inv_mwdm, coeffs_init)` -> the refitted constant values, given the true 1/M_WDM of each simulation. It changes the constants only and leaves the functional form unchanged.

`tree` is a dict holding the merger tree of one simulation: the SubLink tree of its Milky-Way-mass host at z=0, with the subhalos that hold stars or star-forming gas across all 91 outputs. It maps each name in `PROPERTIES` to a numpy array over the nodes, exactly as stored in the tree file and in its units: a scalar field as a 1D array, a vector field as an (N, d) array. The nodes arrive in the depth-first order of the SubLink tree. The linkage fields SubhaloID, DescendantID, FirstProgenitorID, NextProgenitorID, MainLeafProgenitorID, LastProgenitorID and SnapNum are always delivered, whether or not `PROPERTIES` lists them. `stats` stacks the `compute_statistics` outputs of several simulations into an (n_sims, len(STATISTICS)) array. `predict_inv_mwdm` predicts each simulation from its own statistics alone and implements exactly the model stated in mechanism.md.

Besides these statistics and your fitted constants, the file may hold only physical constants and the values fixed across all simulations (Omega_m = 0.302, Omega_b = 0.046, sigma_8 = 0.839, h = 0.6909). No varied simulation parameter enters anywhere. The file is deterministic, imports only numpy and scipy, computes everything from its arguments alone, and contains nothing but the model and its fitting. `compute_statistics` returns within ten seconds for one tree of 100000 nodes, `predict_inv_mwdm` within one minute for 1024 simulations, and `fit_coeffs` within ten minutes.
